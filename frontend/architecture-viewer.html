  • Dashboard with key operational metrics
  • Inventory oversight and management
  • Driver coordination and assignments

  • Stock level management and tracking
  • Request processing and fulfillment
  • Distribution tracking
  • Driver route coordination
  • Historical data and reporting

4. Inventory Manager Module - Weight Recording

  • Fresh tea leaf weight recording
  • Processed bag weight tracking
  • Complete inventory history
  • Real-time updates and synchronization

5. Payment Manager Module - Financial Processing

  • Payment processing and disbursement
  • Advance payment management
  • Loan administration
  • Tea rate configuration
  • Payment history and reports

6. Transport Manager Module - Logistics

  • Route creation and management
  • Driver assignment and scheduling
  • Vehicle tracking
  • Schedule coordination

7. Supplier Module - Tea Supply

  • Tea supply submission portal
  • Supply history tracking
  • Payment status monitoring
  • Performance metrics

8. Driver Module - Delivery Management

  • Delivery status updates
